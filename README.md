A colossal phoenix made of living fire and glowing embers bursts out of an erupting volcano in the dead of night, wings spread wide across a starry sky while rivers of molten lava run glowing down the mountain — and at the very base, one tiny village of warm little lights, so small you feel how enormous the bird really is. Fire, scale, and one impossible moment, all at once.

## Run

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

This copies `entry.png` to `out/entry.png`.
